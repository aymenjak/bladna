## Why these fit (Oasis Modern, DESIGN.md §11)

- **Palette** — `dashboard/01` (MediCore) uses a calm jade family
  (`#105952`/`#0B2F2D`) that sits right next to our `brand-700 #18664a` /
  `brand-900 #0f4030`; `dashboard/02` pairs nature greens with cream surfaces,
  echoing our warm `sand-50 #f7f4ed` page ground.
- **Structure** — the platform shots all follow the anatomy DESIGN.md §4
  already mandates (eyebrow → headline → single search/CTA bar → proof strip →
  cards), useful for validating the landing rhythm rather than reinventing it.
- **Dashboard** — hairline stat grids, icon-led quick links and a quiet
  sidebar are exactly what the P8 `StatGrid`/bento surfaces do; these shots are
  a cross-check for density and chart treatment, not a template to copy.
