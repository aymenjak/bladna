# Photo credits

Every photo in `apps/web/public/photos/` is a real photo **from Algeria**,
sourced from Wikimedia Commons (mostly Wiki Loves Africa entries), resized to
max 1280px / JPEG q82. Licences are CC BY / CC BY-SA (plus one public-domain
image), so **attribution is mandatory**: the public page `/[locale]/credits`
lists author, licence and source for each file, rendered from
`apps/web/src/ui/photo-credits.ts` (data mirrored in `photo-picks-algeria.json`).

Rules: covers are thematic art (`alt=""`, decorative), never a documentary
claim about a specific initiative. Any replacement must carry an open licence
(CC0 / PD / CC BY / CC BY-SA) and be added to both files above.

`how-certificate.jpg` is unchanged: CC0, rawpixel — Certificate Proficiency (24 October 1918).
