# 03 — Risk assessment and treatment register

ISO/IEC 27001:2022 clauses 6.1.2, 6.1.3, 8.2, 8.3

## Method

Likelihood and impact are scored 1 (low) to 3 (high); **risk = likelihood × impact**.
Risks scoring 6 or more must be treated before production launch. Residual
scores assume the listed controls are in place.

## Risk acceptance

Residual risks scoring 3 or less are accepted by the information security owner.
Risks marked _priority_ must be closed before the platform processes real users'
data.
